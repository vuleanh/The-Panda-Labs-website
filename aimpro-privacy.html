AimPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

AimPro ("the app") is provided by The Panda Labs. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

AimPro stores your crosshair presets, visual preferences, language selection, onboarding status, and selected game package names locally on your device. The Panda Labs does not receive this information, and the app does not require an account.

Advertising

AimPro uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Learn more about how Google handles information in its services in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

AimPro requests permission to display a user-controlled crosshair over games and other apps. It may also request notification permission so the active overlay can be managed from a persistent notification. These permissions are used only to provide the app's crosshair functionality.

Children

AimPro is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Security and retention

App preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Information transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit according to Google's documentation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact anhvu.mobileapp@gmail.com.
